Rules at a Glance

The 13 binding rules from README.md that govern every module, component, and AI-generated layout built on the Equinet Design System.

# Principle Binding Specification
1 Tokens only Every colour, size, weight, tracking, spacing, radius and shadow comes from tokens.css. A value not in there is a defect, not a choice.
2 No invented hues The palette is complete for UI. Distinguish items by position, weight, icon or an existing tint pairing — never a new colour. Two named exceptions: chart and diagram categories for marketing artwork only.
3 Three typefaces, no fourth Open Sans headings, Source Sans 3 body, Playfair Display as an optional italic accent.
4 Section headings --equinet-fontsize-section-heading (36px), uppercase, with --equinet-shadow-heading. Weight follows family — 800 Open Sans, 900 body family. Not --equinet-fontsize-h2 (48px).
5 Editor-owned padding & background Never hardcoded past override. Exposed in Module Style settings, injected at render.
6 Full-bleed background parallax Via IntersectionObserver + requestAnimationFrame. Never background-attachment: fixed.
7 Motion needs both halves Declared inside prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, withdrawn inside reduce.
8 Icons — use generously Inline SVG only. Equinet's own first (2px stroke); otherwise Lucide at stroke-width="1" with a <!-- lucide/name --> comment. Nothing over 48px.
9 Illustrations are reference Recompose freely — crop, scale, overlap, extract, rearrange. Colour, typography and iconography still follow the system.
10 Every section gets a visual Place a whole illustration when its subject matches (assets/illustrations/INDEX.md); otherwise compose a bespoke graphic from structural primitives.
11 Ground & ratio discipline scenes/ are opaque; sectors/, systems/, flows/ are transparent and need a surface; pictonodes/ are square and never sit in a 16:9 row.
12 WCAG 2.1 AA Measured not estimated. One signed-off exception: the primary button's white label.
13 Say so rather than approximate If a rule can't be met — no matching illustration, no icon geometry, no token for a value — state it plainly instead of filling the gap with something plausible.

Staggered Content Pattern

.eq-stagger Alternating Rows

Built into components/bundle.css. Uses :nth-child grid ordering so DOM reading order stays intact while media alternates left and right.

Modifier: .eq-stagger__media--scene

Opaque Scene Panels Fill Edge-to-Edge

The 12 illustrations in assets/illustrations/scenes/ carry an opaque navy gradient across the full 641 × 360 artboard. Using .eq-stagger__media--scene ensures zero padding so no page surface leaks around the dark rectangle.

Browse All 12 Scene Illustrations

Modifier: .eq-stagger__media--transparent

Transparent Diagrams Sit on Surface-Alt

Files in sectors/, systems/, and flows/ have transparent artboards. Applying .eq-stagger__media--transparent places them on --equinet-color-surface--alt (#F6F5F5) with 40px tokenised padding.

Modifier: .eq-stagger__row--compact

Square 118 × 118 Pictonodes Pair Inline

Square spot graphics in pictonodes/ never sit in a 16:9 media frame. The .eq-stagger__row--compact modifier sets a 118px 1fr grid column template so the pictonode sits at native scale beside the copy.
