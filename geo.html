Start with the fundamentals

What is Generative Engine Optimisation?

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the practice of making your organisation's expertise and information easier for generative AI systems to discover, interpret and use when responding to relevant questions.

It starts with useful, clear information

Answer real buyer questions directly. Explain your products, services, expertise and terminology in language that people and machines can understand.

It builds on sound search foundations

GEO does not replace SEO. It works alongside accessible pages, helpful content, a technically sound website and consistent information about your organisation.

SEO

Helps search engines discover, crawl and rank relevant pages.

+
GEO

Helps answer engines interpret your expertise and draw on relevant information in generated responses.

A practical approach

How do you improve your visibility in AI search?

Start with a baseline, fix the foundations and improve the information that matters to your audience. Review the evidence and refine the work over time.

Step 01

Assess

Review priority buyer questions, current search visibility, content coverage and how accurately your business is described.

Step 02

Clarify

Align positioning, terminology, service descriptions and the evidence that supports your expertise.

Step 03

Optimise

Improve page structure, answer-led content, internal linking, technical accessibility and relevant structured data.

Step 04

Learn and refine

Monitor representative prompts and available referral or engagement signals, then prioritise the next improvements.

Important: AI platforms differ and change over time. This process improves clarity and discoverability; it cannot guarantee a citation, mention or ranking in a generated answer.

Evidence, not assumptions

How should you measure and improve GEO?

Use a repeatable measurement approach. No single metric fully captures AI visibility, and results can vary by platform, prompt, location and time.

  • Mentions and citationsRecord whether your organisation or pages appear for a defined set of relevant prompts.
  • Accuracy of answersCheck whether descriptions of your products, services and expertise are correct.
  • Referral and engagement signalsWhere analytics make them observable, review AI-referred visits and what those visitors do next.
  • Content coverageIdentify buyer questions that remain unanswered or are answered more clearly elsewhere.

Build a useful baseline first

Choose a small, relevant set of buyer questions, record the platform and date, and repeat the checks consistently. Treat observations as directional evidence, not a universal ranking score.

Common questions

What do B2B businesses need to know about GEO?

Clear answers to the practical questions teams often ask when planning for AI-assisted discovery.

Is GEO different from SEO?

Yes, although they overlap. SEO focuses on helping search engines discover and rank pages. GEO focuses on helping generative answer systems interpret and use relevant information. Strong technical SEO, helpful content and clear site structure support both.

What is the difference between GEO and AEO?

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) commonly describes making content useful for systems that provide direct answers. GEO is often used for optimisation in generative AI experiences. The terms overlap and are not applied consistently across the industry, so define the scope of work rather than relying on the label alone.

Does structured data guarantee that an AI system will cite my website?

No. Structured data can help clarify information about a page or entity when implemented correctly, but it does not guarantee crawling, inclusion, citation or a particular position in a generated answer.

How long does GEO take to work?

There is no universal timeline. Discovery, crawling, platform behaviour, competition, content changes and measurement methods all affect what you observe. Establish a baseline and review changes over a suitable period rather than promising a fixed result.

Can we measure AI visibility reliably?

You can track selected signals, such as mentions in a defined prompt set, citations, answer accuracy and measurable referral traffic. These are partial indicators, not a complete or platform-independent measure of visibility.

Should we replace our existing SEO strategy with GEO?

No. Treat GEO as an extension of a sound search and content strategy. Continue to invest in useful content, technical accessibility, clear positioning and a website experience that helps buyers make informed decisions.
